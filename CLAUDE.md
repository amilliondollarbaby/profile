## 4. Brand and design direction

> **Rebrand, Oct 26 2026 (Dione's call):** lighter and more editorial, fashion-magazine typography.
> - **Background:** light gray studio (#EBEBE9; the hero has a soft studio-sweep vignette). Ink is near-black #141414.
> - **Cotton candy pastels are small accents only** (pink #FAD4E4, purple #E4DAF8, yellow #FCF0B4, sky #D2E8FB): marker highlights behind italic words, dots, dashes. Never pastel cards or sections; cards are off-white. **No red, no brown.**
> - **Typography: one family, Bodoni Moda (variable, optical sizes).** Inspired by a Vogue-style spread ("The way *she* wears it"): heavy high-contrast Didone headlines, tightly stacked staggered lines, one or two *italic* words, italic decks with NAMES IN CAPS, tiny spaced-caps credit labels. Only the typography was borrowed, not that spread's design or colours. Inter Tight, DM Serif Display and Caveat are retired.
> - **Name:** Danielle Sellado is her full name; Dione / Dioné is the nickname and brand. Show both.
> - **Photo:** Dione will shoot a portrait in that spirit (seated, light gray studio). The hero has a slot for it.
> - **Layout is a magazine, not a template:** 12-column grid, a running head on every spread (No. 0X · title · Danielle Sellado), hairline rules, image plates with numbered captions, a different composition per spread. No pills, chips, highlighter bars, dashed boxes or résumé cards. Buttons are underlined text links.
> - No hand-drawn notes or tilted cards. The pink scribble heart stays as the signature.
> - Live tokens are in `src/styles/global.css`. The palette and type below are the **old** brand, kept for reference.

### Typography
- **Inter Tight** (400 / 600 / 800): headlines in 800 with tight tracking (about −0.045em), plus body text and labels
- **DM Serif Display**, italic: the emotional half of a headline, e.g. "Most creators are women. / *Most top creators aren't.*"
- **Caveat** (600 / 700): handwritten margin notes, slightly rotated (−4° to +4°)

### The look
- **Editorial meets hand-drawn.** Big confident sans-serif headlines, serif italics for feeling, and Caveat notes plus hand-drawn SVG arrows and scribbles in the margins.
- **Subtle film grain** on backgrounds (an SVG `feTurbulence` overlay at 20–35% opacity). It keeps flat colors from looking digital.
- **Tactile touches:** rotated sticky notes, tilted cards, soft shadows, pill tags at slight angles.
- **The logo:** "Dioné" in DM Serif Display italic plus a pink scribble heart (`brand/dione-logo.svg`, `brand/scribble-heart.svg`). It appears on every carousel slide, and the site should carry it consistently.
- **Precision is non-negotiable.** Dione checks margins and alignment closely. Use a strict grid and consistent outer margins (her 1080px carousels use 80px), and never let text crowd the edges or the logo.

### What to avoid (she has rejected these)
- **Anything "too Claude" or AI-looking:** generic gradients, glossy 3D blobs, stock SaaS layouts, purple-to-blue gradients, emoji-as-icons
- **Flat cartoon illustrations of people.** She rejected one. If you need a figure, use the hand-drawn line-doodle style (see `brand/doodle-skincare-woman.svg`).
- "Too traditional" templated layouts
- Corporate stiffness

## 6. Voice and copy rules
These come from her own feedback. Follow them for every word on the site.
1. **Direct.** No beating around the bush. Short sentences.
2. **Confident, but never bragging or "entitled."** Show the eye and the skill through the work instead of claiming them.
3. **Not pretentious.** No slogans like "laziness, but make it strategic" or "here's my secret".
4. **Not weak.** No hedging like "some days I forget" or "I'm trying". Self-aware is good: *"Still building the habit. Already worth it."* is the right balance.
5. **Professional but warm.** Never corporate.
6. **Relatable and concrete:** real details over abstractions.
7. When copy invites action, **end with a question or a warm invite**, never a pushy command like "Save this!"
8. Her audience is **CEOs**. Every line should make her look capable and trustworthy.
9. **Never invent facts, clients, numbers or testimonials.** Use `[TODO: …]` placeholders and ask her.

## 7. Technical direction (recommended, confirm with Dione)
- **Stack:** Next.js (App Router) or Astro, plus TypeScript
- **Motion:** GSAP (with ScrollTrigger) for scroll animation, Lenis for smooth scrolling
- **Hosting:** Vercel (free tier), with GitHub for version control. This is the same setup planned for BabbiBot.
- **Fonts:** self-host Inter Tight, DM Serif Display and Caveat with `next/font` or Fontsource
- **Must-haves:**
  - Mobile-first. Many CEOs will open the link on a phone.
  - `prefers-reduced-motion` support. Keep animations tasteful; nothing should block reading.
  - Fast: optimized images, lazy-loaded media, Lighthouse 90+
  - Accessible: semantic HTML, alt text, contrast checks (yellow-on-white text fails, so avoid it)
  - SEO and social: page title, meta description and an OG image in her brand
