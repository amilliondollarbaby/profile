# ODD1S

> For the ones who never quite fit the box, so they made their own.

Dione's art-toy brand. Every figure starts as one of her hand-painted canvases and is reimagined as a
collectible vinyl figure (blind-box, designer-toy look), always in **her** art style: her face shapes,
big eyes, blush and painted texture. The figures are her own characters, not copies of anyone's IP.

Two worlds, one girl: who you were when small things made you happy, and who you became when the world
got bigger and stranger.

**Pipeline:** painting → Higgsfield toy concept renders (front, side, back) → Higgsfield image-to-3D (.glb)
→ Blender (clean-up, vinyl materials, base, lights, render) → Higgsfield polish and upscale → a video per world.

## Collection 1: Little Cravings (the Food Series, tiny world)

> Comfort you can wear.

Before the world got loud, everything that mattered fit on a plate. The Little Cravings girls carry their
favourite meal everywhere: in their hair, on their clothes, floating around them. Each sits inside her own
soft purple **halo**, her safe bubble (from the paintings).

| # | Food | Name | Personality | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Sushi | **Maki** | Calm and sleepy, a fish napping on her head | Painting: sushi girl (yellow background) |
| 02 | Ramen | **Naru** | Soft and a little teary from the steam | Painting: ramen girl (green background) |
| 03 | Mandu | **Mandu** | Round, cozy, the hugger of the group | New |
| 04 | Pancake | **Panky** | Sweet and sticky, always stacked with syrup | New |
| 05 | Matcha strawberry | **Ichigo** | Fresh and pretty, the main character | New |

Names for 03–05 are suggestions; Dione to confirm.

**Video:** a tiny table-top world. The camera floats over a giant plate as each girl pops up from her
blind box inside her glowing halo, food drifting around her like snow.

## Collection 2: Route 222 (the Park, grown-up world)

> Where daydreams go when you grow up.

The same softness, grown up. A dreamy afternoon that never ends: brick bridges to nowhere, clouds you can
lean on, a unicorn in the sky, a UFO quietly beaming someone up. A street sign reads **222**; nobody knows
why, everyone feels lucky when they see it. The four girls are named after songs; the 222 sign marks the way.

Friends in the Park: the cat in hair curlers who winks (starry tail), the dog in a beret, the bunny in
pearls, a retro TV playing a channel called *myxx*, a rotary phone that might ring, dice, an 8-ball,
a crosswalk leading nowhere.

| # | Name | Song | Look | Vibe |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Joline** | *Jolene*, Dolly Parton | short hair | calm, the one everyone notices |
| 02 | **Valerie** | *Valerie*, Amy Winehouse | headphones | lives in her own soundtrack |
| 03 | **Sally** | *Mustang Sally*, Wilson Pickett | TBD | fast car, faster heart |
| 04 | **Candy** | *Candy*, Mandy Moore | TBD | sweet but deadly |

Source: Dione's two large surreal canvases (works in progress).

**Video:** slow and surreal. The camera drifts over the brick bridge, through the clouds, past the 222
sign. The girls lie in the grass, the cat winks, the phone rings, and nobody answers.
