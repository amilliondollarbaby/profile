# START HERE (for Dione)

1. Unzip this folder to your Desktop. It is called **ODD1S-studio**.
2. Open the Claude Desktop app -> **Code** -> new session -> **This computer** -> **Choose...** -> pick **ODD1S-studio**.
3. Paste this as your first message:

   Hi Babbi! Read CLAUDE.md in this folder, it has everything from our day together. I'm a beginner.
   First, connect my Blender with /use-blender and check it works. Then we'll bring in my first figures,
   step by step, one click at a time.

That's it. Claude reads CLAUDE.md on its own, so it already knows the story, your art style and the plan.
